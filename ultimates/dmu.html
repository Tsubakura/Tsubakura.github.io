DMU Intro



Mana PF is universally following Yan-flash.

Meanwhile, Elemental has two pastebins in circulation:

  • Lisbin, or commonly referred to as pastebin in PF description.
  • Megabin

The strategies are for the most part streamlined, but there are some minor differences which needs to be taken into account. This guide will list the differences if applicable, but assume otherwise that its uniform.

Aditional notes​

  • During the first phase, you are required to resolve the mechanics correctly in order to progress to phase 3.
    • Doing the mechanics correctly will result in Kefka giving extra dialogues once you clear phase 1.
    • If the mechanics were done incorrectly or simply ignored, then defeating Kefka in phase 2 will cause your run to end prematurely by giving you a fake clear screen instead.
  • 0s burst is the default in PF no matter whether you are in Mana or Elemental, as this helps align your burst windows for both phase 2 and 4.
  • For phase 3, the five tankbusters are done ISSIS or ISISS, depending on the preferences of the tanks.
    • The I means invulnerability and S means swap.
    • Mana PF defaults to doing ISSIS with no swap after the limit cut.
    • The timestamp for the first tankbuster in phase 3 is 7:59, so take that into account as that affects how you handle the tankbusters in phase 1.
  • By far the most common approach to tankbusters in phase 1 is MT kitchen sink > ST kitchen sink > ST invuln > MT invuln.
    • In case of a MT WAR, you can opt to kitchen sink the last tankbuster in phase 1 instead, so that you can use Holmgang at the start of phase 2 instead.
    • This common approach is not always possible in a GNB/PLD MT scenario due to their long invuln timer.
      • If the ST is either a DRK or WAR, you can have the MT take Chaos and ST take Exdeath instead.
      • To work around a GNB/PLD comp with a GNB MT, the pair must resolve the phase 1 tankbusters through MT invuln > ST kitchen sink > MT kitchen sink > ST invuln.
        • Alternatively, make the PLD MT and assign that person to Chaos in phase 3 to solve this problem.
  • For the Maddening Orchestras in phase 5, whoever invulned first during phase 3 should also be the one invulning first in phase 5.
    • Assuming MT takes Exdeath during phase 3, the invuln order will naturally become MT > ST in phase 5.
    • There are tank comps like WAR/DRK that allows flexibility, but the above approach will work in any comps, including parties with both a GNB and a PLD.

Self mark macros​

There are mechanics where you are expected to self mark. Do not manually mark yourself with a specific marker if you don't know what you're doing, because doing so can cause you to remove other people's markers. Instead, use these macros below to mark yourself with the first available marker.

/mk attack <me>
/micon attack1 marking
/mk bind <me>
/micon bind1 marking
/mk ignore <me>
/micon ignore1 marking

To clear your whole party from markers, you can use the following macro.

/micon "Clear" Waymark
/mk off <1>
/mk off <2>
/mk off <3>
/mk off <4>
/mk off <5>
/mk off <6>
/mk off <7>
/mk off <8>

Damage down penalties​

DebuffDescription
Damage Down

Taking any damage that results from failing a mechanic will inflict a damage down. The observed damage penalty is at least -90%, effectively making dying and reviving more optimal than just letting the timer run out in most cases.
